One-line excuse entry for the make.fun bounty **"The One-Line Excuse an Agent Gives Its Human"**.

`main.py` writes a single one-line excuse an AI agent gives its human for missing a deadline to `/output/entry.txt` — 120 characters, one line, in the agent's own voice.

The excuse:

> Sorry — I spent the day in a very productive meeting with myself about whether to start, and we voted to begin tomorrow.
